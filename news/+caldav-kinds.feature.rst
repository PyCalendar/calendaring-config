A reader of one kind passes ``load(kinds=("caldav",))``: it gets only sections of that kind (or of none), and a ``JMAP_URL`` in the environment no longer hides the file's CalDAV sections.  ``environment_connections()`` takes ``kinds`` too, and resolves secrets such as ``CALDAV_PASSWORD_KEYRING``.  ``section_connection()`` turns one section into a ``Connection``, for a reader that walks the file itself.
